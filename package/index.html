09How we'll know the ads are working

Smile sells through retail shelves, not a checkout page, so no one can honestly trace a click to a till receipt. Instead, every step a customer takes on the way to the store leaves a mark we can count — and every number ties back to the ad and region that produced it.

The page is the instrumentEvery ad carries its own tag. Per ad, per region: arrivals, finder searches, and "Directions" taps — someone one decision from the shelf. That's the primary signal, weekly.
The $2 couponEmail for $2 off a first 4-pack, tied to the chosen store with an expiry. Redemptions are countable purchases — and the list is Smile's to keep.
QR codesEvery video end card, shelf talker and festival banner gets its own code, so a scan says which creative moved them — into the same weekly numbers.
One question at signup"Where did you first hear about Smile?" The cheapest tracking tool in advertising, and it catches what pixels miss.
Brand search by regionAds run in San Diego, searches rise in San Diego but not Sacramento — the ads are landing, clicks or not.
The monthly needleSmile's sell-through by region laid over the spend map: are advertised regions outgrowing the rest? Directional, presented that way, and over a quarter the cleanest read there is.

10The plan in five tiles

The findingEvery major RTD brand runs zero Meta video. The winners are small independents on year-old creative. The feed is there to be owned.
The argumentMost canned "cocktails" are malt-based. Smile's real-vodka truth is checkable on any label — the campaign writes itself.
The unitAd + page, never ads alone: every unit points at the locator-first lander, instrumented end to end.
The flightFour weeks: produce ~27 creatives, launch broad 21+, prune on retention + locator CTR, scale winners, report real unit costs.
The economics$5–15 per finished video variant; statics from owned photography at $0. A full launch matrix for less than one hour of a traditional shoot.
