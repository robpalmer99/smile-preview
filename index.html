For the skeptics

We know what you're thinking.

You've been burned by canned drinks before. Chemical aftertaste. Fake sweetness. We get it. Here's why Smile is different.

“Canned cocktails all taste fake.”

Most are malt-based — brewed like beer and dressed up in cocktail flavoring. Smile is built from real vodka, real juice and sparkling water. Nothing to cover up, so there's no cover-up taste.

“I can make my own vodka cranberry.”

We bet you can, and it's delicious. But try doing it at the beach, on the golf course, or for twelve people at a barbecue. Smile is for all the places your bar cart can't follow you.

“$12.99 for four?”

That's $3.25 a cocktail, made with real vodka. One round at a bar costs more than the whole 4-pack — and you've tasted what the cheap cans are hiding.

“Why is there a smiley face on the can?”

Because that's the whole point — the moment you crack a drink with a friend and smile. If you're going to call your brand Smile, it had better be a fun one.

“Is 7% a lot?”

It's the strength of a properly made single cocktail — one can, one real drink, built right. Not a watered-down seltzer, not a syrupy heavyweight.
